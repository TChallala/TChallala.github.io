# Client-First Portfolio Design

## Objective

Reposition the portfolio so a prospective client immediately understands what Teofilo can do for their business, while retaining enough technical depth for recruiters and engineering teams.

## Audience

- Small and medium businesses needing a new or improved website.
- Clients needing WordPress, Shopify, or Wix setup, customization, maintenance, or troubleshooting.
- Teams needing custom web applications, APIs, integrations, QA, or ongoing technical support.
- Recruiters evaluating full-stack development and QA experience.

## Messaging Direction

Lead with client outcomes rather than biography or technology names. Teofilo's name and professional title remain visible, but the primary headline explains the value delivered: fast, reliable, responsive, and maintainable websites and web applications.

The copy must remain factual. Do not invent client names, conversion gains, revenue figures, traffic improvements, or other results that are not documented. Do not mention VistaFlix or identify any NDA-covered project.

## Information Architecture

1. Hero: client outcome, concise service summary, and two direct calls to action.
2. Services: four scannable offerings for business websites, custom applications, integrations, and QA/maintenance.
3. Selected Work: existing projects reframed around the problem solved and useful outcome, followed by technical evidence.
4. Experience: delivery background and platform experience.
5. Skills: detailed technical stack for recruiters and technical buyers.
6. About: brief personal context and working style.
7. Contact: invitation to discuss a project or role.

## Hero Copy

- Eyebrow: `Teofilo Gonzaga III — Full-Stack Developer & QA Engineer`
- Headline: `Websites and web applications that are fast, reliable, and easy to manage.`
- Full-stack message: explain responsive websites, WordPress/Shopify/Wix work, custom applications, testing, and clear communication.
- QA message: explain dependable releases through UI, API, regression, and end-to-end testing.
- Primary CTA: `See How I Can Help`
- Secondary CTA: `Discuss Your Project`

## Services

Use four cards:

1. `Business Websites & CMS` — WordPress, Shopify, Wix, responsive layouts, theme customization, and content updates.
2. `Custom Web Applications` — frontend and backend development for booking, management, reporting, and other workflow systems.
3. `APIs & Integrations` — REST APIs, authentication, databases, third-party services, and background processing.
4. `QA, Maintenance & Support` — testing, troubleshooting, performance-minded maintenance, regression checks, and reliable releases.

## Work Presentation

Keep the existing project set and screenshots. Rewrite descriptions so the first sentence explains the user or business need and the second explains the implementation. Do not claim quantitative results without evidence. Keep the production streaming project anonymous and do not add any client-identifying link or brand name.

## Visual Direction

Retain the current dark/light theme, sidebar, typography, blue accent, motion, project galleries, and card language. Add service cards using the existing design tokens and responsive grid conventions. The change is an information and messaging improvement, not a visual rebrand.

## Contact Direction

Use project-oriented language without excluding recruiters. The contact section should welcome website projects, custom application work, maintenance, QA engagements, and full-time opportunities. Do not publish a WhatsApp number unless Teofilo separately approves putting it on the public portfolio.

## Constraints

- HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript only.
- Preserve accessibility, keyboard behavior, mobile navigation, theme switching, galleries, and role switching.
- Preserve existing uncommitted user changes.
- No commits.
- No NDA-covered names, URLs, screenshots, or identifying details.
